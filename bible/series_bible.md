# Otterly Curious: Series Bible
Tagline: Every big question starts with "Let's find out!"
Audience: ages 3-7, co-viewed. Format: 6-8 min episodes + two vertical Shorts each.
Core promise: one real kid question, one adventure, one value shown through action, one song.
Tone: warm, funny, gentle. Humor from character (Gus's shortcuts). No villains, scares, yelling, humans, screens, or brands.

## World: Driftwood Cove
Small sunny harbor on a rocky coast. Fixed locations (see bible/locations/): The Kelp Shallows, The Tide Pools,
Gran Mari's Lighthouse Point, The Driftwood Playground, The Wonder Shed. A buoy or fishing boat may pass far away; nobody is seen aboard.

## Episode spine (7 beats)
1 Cold open 0:00-0:15 | 2 Theme 0:15-0:45 | 3 Question in play 0:45-2:30 | 4 It goes wrong 2:30-3:30 |
5 Find out (at-home demo) 3:30-5:15 | 6 Put it to use 5:15-6:15 | 7 Wonder Pocket recap 6:15-7:00.
Script rules: one Big Question phrased as a child asks it; real, safe, household-item demo; value lands through action;
age-5 reading level; no Ollie/Pip/Gus/Bean sentence over 12 words; every science answer true at kid level.

## Known character issues (carry into prompts)
- Bean's close-up face is very close to Ollie's; keep her ribbon/kelp blanket in frame and her body smaller.
- Gran Mari's head reads bald; keep scarf and glasses visible and avoid extreme close-ups until redesigned.
- Ollie's tuft leans back; "curious" beats should be keyed to the tuft going up in animation or an edit.
