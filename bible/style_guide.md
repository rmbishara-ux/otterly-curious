# Style Guide (v2, soft 3D, replaces cut-paper v1)
Look: soft glossy 3D animation render, toy-like rounded forms, oversized round heads, huge glossy eyes with big whites,
rosy cheeks, smooth fur/feather/skin surfaces, soft studio lighting, subtle specular highlights, shallow depth of field.
No sharp edges, no fine fur strands, no dark or scary lighting.

Palette (approximate, eyeballed from approved sheets; sample exact values per region before QA automation):
Cove Teal-Blue fur ~#4F8FA3 | Otter belly Cream ~#F1E3C6 | Wonder Yellow vest ~#D9A441 | Rock Grey ~#9AA0AE |
Pip Orange ~#E98B5A | Teacup Sky Blue ~#7CB8EA | Gran Mari Brown ~#9A6B56, Scarf Coral ~#E98873 |
Gus White ~#F4F5FA, Wing Grey ~#B8BCCB, Sneaker Red ~#D83A3A, Beak Yellow ~#F2C94C |
Bean Ribbon Lavender ~#B79AE0, Kelp Green ~#7DAA4F | Luna Violet ~#CDBDEB with white glow | Background studio light-blue ~#DCE6F7.
Glow rule: Luna's glow is the only emissive/glowing element.

Backgrounds: five fixed plates, each with far/mid/near layers; variants: morning, golden hour, night (lighthouse beam).
Typography: Big Question on screen only, rounded sans (Nunito or Fredoka).
Camera: mostly static or slow pans; characters move in simple arcs.
Reference rule: every shot prompt = style prefix + character reference image + scene + action + camera. Never freehand a character.
Hard rules: original characters only; no humans; no brands, logos, or existing characters in any prompt; do not name
real artists or channels in prompts.
