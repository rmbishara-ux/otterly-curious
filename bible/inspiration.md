# Design inspiration: CoComelon (UCbCmjCuTUZos6Inko4u57UQ)

Reference only. Per the brief's hard rules we never name, imitate, or reproduce its characters, songs, or art style.
Scale for context: 203M subs, 229B views, 2,163 uploads (vidIQ, Oct 26 2026).

## What we take (structure, not look)
- **Short core runtime:** the top ~45 videos are 2:05 to 4:30. Anything over 30 min is a compilation. Our 6-8 min episodes sit
  between song-length and compilation, so every beat must earn its place. Cut Shorts from beat 5 and beat 4.
- **Title formula:** `[Plain topic] | [Channel] ...`. Topic first, instantly legible. Ours: Big Question first, `| Otterly Curious` last, under 60 chars.
- **Song as retention engine:** nearly every top video is a song. We keep the 30s theme and recap jingle as the repeatable hook.
- **Everyday, kid-scale topics** (bath, sick, swimming, clean up). Ours stay everyday science questions kids actually ask.
- **Comments disabled (commentCount 0)** matches our made-for-kids settings.
- **Large readable faces, big eyes, one clear emotion per frame.** Applies to our expression sheets and thumbnails.
- **Evergreen catalog beats virality:** the top videos are 2017-2019 and still compound.

## What we deliberately do NOT take
- Glossy 3D, hyper-saturated color, constant cutting. Ours is flat cut-paper, locked 8-color palette, slow pans.
- The nursery-rhyme loop / compilation format. Ours is one question, one story, one lesson.
- Any character, melody, or scene from the channel.

## Style direction change (Oct 26, owner decision)
Owner dislikes the flat cut-paper look. New direction: soft, glossy 3D-rendered look inspired by the traits below.
Traits observed in the reference channel's thumbnails (traits only; no characters, logos, or scenes are reproduced):
- Oversized round head on a small body, toy-like rounded forms, no sharp edges
- Very large glossy eyes with big whites, one clear emotion per frame
- Smooth soft skin/fur surfaces, rosy cheeks, soft specular highlights
- Bright saturated primaries against clean, simple backgrounds, shallow depth of field
- Candy-like props with glossy plastic sheen
This SUPERSEDES the brief's locked cut-paper style and 8-color palette (palette.json is now legacy until a new palette is chosen).
Still locked: original animal cast, no humans, no existing characters/brands, no logos in prompts or art.
