## Captions
1 (Fri Oct 30): Gus has a faster way to bring the sea back. What could go wrong? 🦅🌊 Our first episode is live tomorrow. Link in bio.
2 (Sat Oct 31): Where did the water go? 🌊 Here's the at-home experiment from our first episode: set a wide bowl of water on a table, tilt it very slowly, and watch the water climb one side and slide away from the other. It's just like the tide. Full story on YouTube, link in bio.
3 (Tue Nov 3): Why do otters hold hands? 🦦 Float two pairs of leaves in a bowl, tie one pair together with soft string, then swirl. The loose leaves drift apart, the tied ones stay together. New episode out this morning, link in bio.
4 (Thu Nov 5): Gus tries to take a nap the fast way. The kelp pad has other plans. 😴🦦 Full episode on YouTube, link in bio.
5 (Sat Nov 7): Where does a rainbow come from? 🌈 With a grown-up's help, set a clear glass of water on a sunny windowsill and look at the wall for a tiny rainbow. Nudge the glass and watch it move. New episode out this morning, link in bio.
6 (Sun Nov 8): Gus caught a rainbow! (Or did he?) 🌈🧢 Full story on YouTube, link in bio.
