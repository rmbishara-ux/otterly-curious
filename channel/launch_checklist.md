# Launch checklist
- [ ] Owner connects @OtterlyCurious123 to vidIQ (link provided in chat) so assets/metadata can be pushed, or applies the kit manually.
- [ ] Verify the channel by phone (needed for >15 min videos and custom thumbnails).
- [ ] Upload avatar (branding/avatar_800.png), banner (branding/banner_2560x1440.png), watermark (branding/watermark_150.png, display for the whole video).
- [ ] Paste description, keywords and contact from about.md; set country and language.
- [ ] Set channel audience to Made for Kids; confirm Shorts default too.
- [ ] Create playlists from playlists.md.
- [ ] Voices and music: provider chosen, voice IDs saved in bible/voice_configs.json, licenses logged.
- [ ] Bank at least 3 finished episodes before the first public date (2026-12-05 in youtube/content_calendar.md).
- [ ] Each upload: title/description/tags from episodes/EXX/youtube.md, captions from final/captions.srt, thumbnail from final/EXX_thumbnail.png, scheduled for Saturday 9:00 AM PT.
- [ ] Monetization: check current YouTube Partner Program thresholds in Studio (earlier tiers require roughly 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 public watch hours; full tier 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours; verify, as they change). Made-for-kids means contextual ads only, no comments, no personalized ads.
- [ ] Keep the audit log (script version, asset provenance, QA report) per episode in this repo.

## Policy posture (YouTube's rules on repetitive/mass-produced content)
Original characters, original scripts with distinct questions, locations and demos per episode, real human editorial control, QA gates, and a documented audit trail. Do not scale volume past what can be reviewed by a person.
