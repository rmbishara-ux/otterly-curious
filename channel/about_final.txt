Every big question starts with "Let's find out!"

Join Ollie the sea otter, Pip the hermit crab, Gran Mari the pelican, Gus the seagull, baby Bean, and glowing Luna in Driftwood Cove. Each story answers one real question kids ask, like why the tide goes out, where rainbows come from, and why boats float. Every episode ends with a safe experiment you can try at home with a bowl, a flashlight, or a seed in a jar.

Made for ages 3 to 7 and best watched together. Gentle, funny, and never scary. Each episode also teaches a kind habit like patience, honesty, or sharing.

New episode every Saturday morning. "Try this at home" clips on Tuesdays and funny Gus clips on Thursdays.

Characters, stories and experiments are original. Voices and music are created with AI tools, and every episode is reviewed by a person before it is published.
