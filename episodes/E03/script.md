# E03: Gus and the Stolen Rainbow
big_question: Where does a rainbow come from?
value: Sharing
demo: A glass of water on a sunny windowsill throws a rainbow on the wall.
book_title: Gus and the Stolen Rainbow
bean_word: Share!
science: Sunlight looks plain, but it is made of all the colors mixed together. Water bends the light and spreads the colors apart, so we see a rainbow.
demo_steps: With a grown-up's help, fill a clear glass with water. Set it on a sunny windowsill so the sun shines through it. Look at the wall or floor nearby and find the little rainbow. Nudge the glass a little and watch the rainbow move.
audit_note: Sunlight really is all the colors together, and water really bends each color a different amount, which spreads them into a rainbow. The glass of water is a small stand-in for the raindrops in the sky, and the script says "tiny drops of rain work like this glass." It skips the angle and the inside bounce of a raindrop. The script says to never look straight at the sun.
