# E10: Pip's Brave New Teacup
big_question: Why does Pip need a new shell?
value: Courage and change
demo: Try on shoes that are too small, then shoes that fit.
book_title: Pip's Brave New Teacup
bean_word: Brave!
science: Hermit crabs grow, so they move into bigger shells when their old one gets too small.
demo_steps: With a grown-up, find an old pair of shoes you wore when you were smaller. Try to put them on. Wiggle your toes and notice how squished they feel. Now put on the shoes you wear today. Wiggle your toes again. Notice how much roomier they are. That is just what a hermit crab feels like in a shell that is too small, and in one that fits.
audit_note: Hermit crabs really do outgrow their shells and move into a bigger one, often a found shell. Pip uses a teacup because he is a storybook crab, so the cup stands in for a shell and the script keeps calling it a shell-cup. Real crabs change shells quickly and sometimes with other crabs nearby, but the script shows the swap behind a kelp curtain to keep it gentle and does not show the soft crab body. The shoes are a model of "too small, then fits"; it is not a claim that crabs wear shoes, and the script says "pretend" out loud. Pip's character sheet needs a new-teacup version (slightly bigger, with a lid) for Season 2. The old chipped sky-blue cup stays in the Wonder Shed as a keepsake.
