TRY IT AT HOME: With a grown-up, find an old pair of shoes you wore when you were smaller. Try to put them on. Wiggle your toes and notice how squished they feel. Now put on the shoes you wear today. Wiggle your toes again. Notice how much roomier they are. That is just what a hermit crab feels like in a shell that is too small, and in one that fits.

In this episode of Otterly Curious, Ollie the sea otter and his friends ask: Why does Pip need a new shell? Hermit crabs grow, so they move into bigger shells when their old one gets too small. Along the way, Gus learns about courage and change.

A science-for-kids story for ages 3-7, made to be watched together.
