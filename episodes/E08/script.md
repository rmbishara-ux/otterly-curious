# E08: Ollie and the Lights-Out Song
big_question: Why do we have to sleep?
value: Listening to your body
demo: A yawn-chain game, and a pretend rubber-band body that is gently stretched and then rested.
book_title: Luna's Lights-Out Song
bean_word: Sleep!
science: Sleep gives our bodies time to rest and grow, and gives our brains time to sort out the day.
demo_steps: With a grown-up, hold a rubber band and stretch it just a little, then let it go slack. Pretend it is your body after a busy day. Now set it down and rest it for a minute. Pick it up and see it is soft and ready again. Then yawn and see who yawns with you.
audit_note: The rubber band is a pretend body, and the script says "pretend" out loud. It shows that things work better after a rest, but it is a model and not how bodies actually repair. Real sleep is when bodies do a lot of resting and growing and when brains sort out what happened that day, so "rest, grow, and sort the day" is true at kid level. Yawn-catching is real and fun but scientists are not sure why it spreads, so the script only calls it a game and never explains it. The stretch is gentle only and a grown-up helps.

## BEAT 5 | Find out | 3:30-5:15 | location=lighthouse_point | time=night | mood=cozy
> The friends climb the path to the lighthouse. Warm yellow light spills from the window. Gran Mari rocks on her porch, knitting a long coral sock. {gran_mari=happy; cam=pan_up}
GRAN_MARI (happy): Well now, such a yawny parade. What brings you up so late?
OLLIE (curious): Gran Mari, why do we have to sleep?
GRAN_MARI (happy): Oh, that is a wonderful question, Ollie. Let's find out together.
> Gran Mari reaches into her pouch and pulls out a plain rubber band and a little wooden box. Pip climbs onto the porch rail for a closer look. {gran_mari=calm, pip=curious; cam=close; dur=5}
GRAN_MARI (calm): This band is a pretend body. Our bodies work hard all day. Running, jumping, bouncing on kelp.
> She holds the band between two wings and stretches it, only a little, then lets it go loose. {cam=top_down; dur=6}
GRAN_MARI (calm): See? A busy day stretches us. Gentle, now. Never pull it far.
OLLIE (curious): Is it tired?
GRAN_MARI (calm): A bit. Now we let it rest in the box. Let's all be very quiet.
> She sets the band in the little box and shuts the lid. The friends hold still. Gus tries, then hiccups. {gus=worried, ollie=calm, pip=calm; cam=close; dur=5}
> Luna floats over the box. Her glow dips low and slow, like a lullaby. {luna=calm; glow=dim; cam=close; dur=5}
GRAN_MARI (happy): Well now, what do you think happens next?
OLLIE (curious): It gets all rested and ready again?
> Gran Mari opens the box. The band lies soft and relaxed, ready to go. Everyone leans in. {cam=top_down; dur=6}
GRAN_MARI (calm): Yes. Sleep is how our bodies rest and grow. While we rest, our bodies do big jobs.
PIP (curious): Like growing taller? Even while we're not moving?
GRAN_MARI (happy): Just so. And our brains sort out the day, like tidying a toy box.
> A cozy picture bubble: a little brain tidying shells, feathers, and stars into small boxes, one by one. {cam=close; dur=6}
OLLIE (surprised): So sleep isn't wasted time. It's busy time!
> Luna rises above the box and sparkles. Three soft notes ring out. {luna=happy; glow=sparkle; ollie=happy, pip=happy; cam=close}
GUS (worried): So when I yawn, my body is saying...
GRAN_MARI (calm): It is saying, "Please, I need a rest." It is good to listen.
> Gus looks at his own wing, then at his sneakers. Quietly, he takes his sneakers off. {gus=sorry; cam=close}
> Ollie turns to the camera, holding the rubber band and the wonder rock. Behind him the lamp sweeps slow across the sea. {ollie=happy; cam=close; dur=6}
OLLIE (happy): Try it at home! Ask a grown-up to help. Stretch a rubber band a tiny bit. Pretend it's your body after a busy day. Rest it for a minute. See how soft it is? Now yawn and see who yawns with you!
