# E01: Ollie and the Vanishing Water
big_question: Why does the water go away?
value: Patience
demo: Bowl of water, tilt it slowly to show high and low.
book_title: Ollie and the Vanishing Water
bean_word: Wait!
science: The moon gently pulls on the ocean, so the water rises up the beach and slides back out two times a day.
demo_steps: Set a wide bowl of water on a table. Tilt it very slowly and watch the water climb one side and slide away from the other. Tilt it back and watch the water return.
audit_note: The bowl is a pretend ocean; the script says so out loud. It shows water moving up and down, not the real tidal bulge.

## BEAT 3 | The question in play | 0:45-2:30 | location=tide_pools | time=morning | mood=wonder
> The friends reach the tide pools. The sea is still far away. Bean floats in the biggest pool, her kelp blanket on her tummy. {ollie=curious, pip=worried, bean=happy; cam=static}
PIP (worried): Maybe the sea is hiding.
OLLIE (curious): Maybe it went on a trip!

PIP (curious): Even the snails are leaving.
> Gus lands with a flap, red sneakers skidding in the sand, a crumb stuck to his big beak. {gus=excited; cam=pan_right}
GUS (excited): I know! The sea ran away. Easy!
OLLIE (curious): Why would the sea run away?
GUS (playful): Maybe it got bored. Or hungry!
> Pip hides a little deeper in the pocket. Bean hums and waves her paws at a starfish. {pip=worried, bean=happy; cam=close}
PIP (worried): I don't think seas get hungry.
> Ollie and Pip study a line of wavy ripples in the sand, tracing them with a paw. {ollie=curious, pip=curious; cam=close; dur=5}
OLLIE (curious): The sand is shaped like waves. So the water was here!
> Ollie checks his four pockets one by one: a shell, a feather, a bit of sea glass, and the wonder rock. {ollie=determined; cam=close}
OLLIE (determined): I'll go find it. Follow me!
> Ollie dashes across the wet sand. Pip bounces in the pocket. Bean is left floating alone. {ollie=excited, pip=worried, bean=curious; cam=pan_right}
PIP (worried): Ollie, wait! Bean is still back there!

GUS (excited): Ha! I've got a faster way!
PIP (worried): Gus, your faster ways always go wrong.
GUS (playful): Not this time! We bring the sea back ourselves!

GUS (excited): Buckets! Lots and lots of buckets!
> Bean claps her paws and floats in a happy circle. Luna bobs along behind her. {bean=happy, luna=calm; glow=dim; cam=close; dur=5}
> Gus waddles toward the far sea, dragging a bright blue bucket by its handle. {gus=excited; cam=pan_right}
PIP (curious): What if we try it the careful way?
OLLIE (curious): Let's watch Gus try first. Then we ask Gran Mari.
> The friends follow Gus across the shiny wet sand, footprints behind them. {ollie=happy, pip=worried, bean=happy; cam=follow}

## BEAT 4 | It goes wrong | 2:30-3:30 | location=tide_pools | time=morning | mood=uh-oh
> Gus fills his bucket at the edge of the faraway sea and trots back, water sloshing. {gus=determined; cam=follow}
GUS (excited): See? One bucket, one big splash!
> He tips the bucket into a tide pool. The water spreads thin and soaks into the sand. {gus=excited; cam=close}
GUS (worried): Hey! Where did it go?
> Gus hurries back for another bucket. His red sneakers hit a slippery rock. {gus=surprised; cam=wide}
GUS (surprised): Whoa, whoa, whoaaa!
> Splash! Gus slides into the pool and sits down in the middle, bucket on his head. A starfish looks on. {gus=surprised; cam=wide; dur=6}
> Ollie, Pip, and Bean try very hard not to giggle. Luna wobbles with a tiny glow. {ollie=happy, pip=happy, bean=happy, luna=calm; glow=dim; cam=close}
PIP (surprised): Are you okay, Gus?
GUS (excited): I'm fine! I did that on purpose!
OLLIE (curious): Did you slip, Gus?
GUS (worried): No! I was testing the water. It's wet. Very wet.
> Gus climbs out dripping, feathers flat, his one feather still sticking up. He wrings out a sneaker. {gus=worried; cam=close}
OLLIE (curious): Your bucket water sank into the sand.
GUS (worried): It was already wet, so it doesn't count!
PIP (curious): Maybe buckets can't bring the sea back.

> The friends look at the tiny dark patch of sand. The big sea is still far away. {ollie=worried, pip=worried, bean=curious; cam=slow_push}
OLLIE (worried): We need help. Let's ask Gran Mari.

## BEAT 5 | Find out | 3:30-5:15 | location=lighthouse_point | time=morning | mood=cozy
> The friends climb the path to the lighthouse. Gran Mari rocks on her porch, knitting, her coral scarf swinging. {gran_mari=happy; cam=pan_up}
GRAN_MARI (happy): Well now, a soggy seagull and a worried otter. What's all this?
OLLIE (curious): Gran Mari, why does the water go away?
GRAN_MARI (happy): Oh, that is a good question, Ollie. Let's find out together.
> Pip climbs out of Ollie's pocket onto the porch rail and peeks over the edge of the empty bowl. {pip=curious; cam=close; dur=5}
> Gran Mari sets a wide bowl of water on the porch rail. The friends crowd around. {gran_mari=calm, ollie=curious, pip=curious; cam=close}
GRAN_MARI (calm): This bowl is a pretend ocean. Watch the water, and watch slowly.
> She tilts the bowl just a little. Water climbs up one side and slides away from the other. {cam=top_down; dur=7}
OLLIE (surprised): It moves! High on one side, low on the other!
> A pale moon hangs in the blue morning sky above the lighthouse. {cam=tilt_up}
GRAN_MARI (calm): See the moon? It pulls on the sea, just a little. Like a very gentle tug.
PIP (curious): So the moon makes the water go up and down?
GRAN_MARI (happy): Yes. The water rises up the beach. Then it slides back out. Two times every day.
> Time-lapse from the porch: waves creep up the sand, then slide away, then creep up again. {cam=timelapse; dur=7}
OLLIE (excited): The water didn't run away! It's just out for a while!
> Ollie hugs the wonder rock and spins in a happy circle. Bean giggles on the rail. {ollie=happy, bean=happy; cam=wide; dur=5}
> Luna rises above the bowl and sparkles. Three soft notes ring out. {luna=happy; glow=sparkle; ollie=happy, pip=happy; cam=close}
GUS (curious): So how long until it comes back?
GRAN_MARI (curious): Well now, what do you think happens next?
OLLIE (curious): It slides back to the beach!
> Gran Mari tilts the bowl the other way. The water flows back across the bowl. {cam=top_down; dur=6}
GRAN_MARI (calm): The sea keeps its own clock. We can't hurry it. But we can watch it.
> Gus looks at his empty bucket, then sets it down very gently. {gus=sorry; cam=close}
> Ollie turns to the camera, holding the wonder rock. Behind him the bowl wobbles. {ollie=happy; cam=close; dur=6}
OLLIE (happy): Try it at home! Put water in a bowl. Tilt it very slowly. Watch the water climb one side. Watch it slide away from the other.

## BEAT 6 | Put it to use | 5:15-6:15 | location=driftwood_playground | time=golden | mood=triumph
> Back at the beach. Ollie pushes a driftwood stick into the sand right where the water ends. {ollie=determined; cam=wide}
OLLIE (determined): This is our water line. We wait and we watch.
> The friends build a sandcastle next to the stick, taking turns with the bucket. Gus pats the sand gently. {ollie=happy, pip=happy, bean=happy, gus=playful; cam=wide}
> Pip stacks tiny shells along the castle wall, one careful shell at a time. {pip=happy; cam=close; dur=5}
> Bean pats the top tower with both paws. Ollie smiles at her. {bean=happy, ollie=happy; cam=close; dur=5}
> Shadows grow long. The sun slides lower. Far away, the sea starts to shine. {cam=timelapse; dur=6}
GUS (excited): I can't wait anymore! I'll go get the sea myself!
BEAN (worried): Wait!

> The first wave slides over the sand and fills the moat of the sandcastle. {cam=wide; dur=6}

OLLIE (excited): Gran Mari was right! We waited and watched!
