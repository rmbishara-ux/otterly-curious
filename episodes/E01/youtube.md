## Description
TRY IT AT HOME: Set a wide bowl of water on a table. Tilt it very slowly and watch the water climb one side and slide away from the other. Tilt it back and watch the water return.

In this episode of Otterly Curious, Ollie the sea otter and his friends ask: Why does the water go away? The moon gently pulls on the ocean, so the water rises up the beach and slides back out two times a day. Along the way, Gus learns about patience.

A science-for-kids story for ages 3-7, made to be watched together.
