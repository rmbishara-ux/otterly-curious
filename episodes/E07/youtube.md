## Description
> NOTE: chapter times are estimates from the storyboard; regenerate after the final render.

TRY IT AT HOME: Ask a grown-up to help. Fill a tub with water. Heap some sand at one end to make a pretend beach. Put clean pretend litter on the sand: a paper cup, a spoon, a bottle cap, a pebble. Gently swish the water like a wave and watch what floats out to sea. Then pick everything out with a pail and sort it into things that float and things that sink. Never touch real litter outside. Tell a grown-up instead.

In this episode of Otterly Curious, Ollie the sea otter and his friends ask: Where does the trash go? Litter left on the beach does not go away. The water carries it into the sea, and it can hurt animals there. We can pick it up, sort it, and take it where it belongs. Along the way, Gus learns about caring for our home.

A science-for-kids story for ages 3-7, made to be watched together.
