# E07: Ollie Cleans the Cove
big_question: Where does the trash go?
value: Caring for our home
demo: A pretend beach in a tub: sand at one end and water at the other. Swish the water like a wave, then sort clean pretend litter into what floats and what sinks, with a grown-up helping.
book_title: Ollie Cleans the Cove
bean_word: Clean!
science: Litter left on the beach does not go away. The water carries it into the sea, and it can hurt animals there. We can pick it up, sort it, and take it where it belongs.
demo_steps: Ask a grown-up to help. Fill a tub with water. Heap some sand at one end to make a pretend beach. Put clean pretend litter on the sand: a paper cup, a spoon, a bottle cap, a pebble. Gently swish the water like a wave and watch what floats out to sea. Then pick everything out with a pail and sort it into things that float and things that sink. Never touch real litter outside. Tell a grown-up instead.
audit_note: The tub is a pretend beach and the script says so out loud. Real litter is a bigger problem than one tub shows, and not all litter floats; the sort-it step shows both floaters and sinkers. We keep it kind-level: litter can hurt animals and we can help by picking it up with a grown-up. Real litter is never touched by the kids in the demo, and the script says to tell a grown-up.
