TRY IT AT HOME: Ask a grown-up to help. Have the grown-up poke one small hole in the bottom of a paper cup. Put a flashlight inside the cup so its light shines out through the hole. Make the room dark. Aim at the wall, never at anyone's eyes. Turn slowly in a circle. The dot sweeps across the wall, then it is gone, then it comes back.

In this episode of Otterly Curious, Ollie the sea otter and his friends ask: Why does the lighthouse blink? Light travels in straight lines. A lighthouse lamp stays on, but it slowly turns, so its beam sweeps past you and away again. That looks like blinking, and it helps boats find the way home. Along the way, Gus learns about honesty.

A science-for-kids story for ages 3-7, made to be watched together.
