# E05: The Day Gus Forgot the Seed
big_question: What does a seed need?
value: Responsibility
demo: A bean seed in a clear bag with a wet paper towel, taped to a sunny window.
book_title: The Day Gus Forgot the Seed
bean_word: Water!
science: A seed wakes up and starts to grow when it gets water and warmth. Then the little plant reaches for light, and it needs time to grow big.
demo_steps: Ask a grown-up to help. Wet a paper towel and fold it. Slip the towel and a bean seed into a clear bag. Tape the bag to a sunny window. Check it every day. Add a few drops of water if the towel dries out. In about a week, a white root and a green shoot appear.
audit_note: Simplified for kids: seeds mostly need water and warmth to wake up, and light matters once the green shoot comes out, so the script says wake up first, then reach for light. A sunny window gives warmth and light together, which is why it is a good spot. Sprouting takes several days, so the script shows it with a week-old bag and a time-lapse. This is a real experiment, not a pretend model.
