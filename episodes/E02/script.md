# E02: Hold On, Bean
big_question: Why do otters hold hands?
value: Sticking together
demo: Two leaves floating on a bowl of water, one pair tied together with a string; the tied pair stays together.
book_title: Hold On, Bean
bean_word: Hold!
science: Sea otters hold paws while they rest and sleep so the moving water cannot drift them apart.
demo_steps: Fill a wide bowl with water. Float two leaves on one side and two leaves on the other. Tie one pair loosely together with a soft string. Gently swirl the water with a finger. Watch the loose leaves drift apart while the tied leaves stay together.
audit_note: Otters really do rest in groups called rafts and hold paws, and mothers hold pups, so the water cannot pull them apart. The string is pretend paws, and the script says so out loud. It shows holding together against drifting, not the whole reason otters do it.
